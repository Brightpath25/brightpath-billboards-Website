Measurement framework

Proven

Definition: Directly recorded operational information.
Source: BrightPath campaign records.
Limitation: Not independent audience exposure or creative proof-of-play.

Estimated

Definition: Modeled or third-party-supported audience information.
Source: Identified external data and disclosed methodology.
Limitation: Not verified reach or a person-level count.

Pilot / Study

Definition: Advanced effectiveness work requiring an agreed study design.
Source: Campaign-specific methodology and qualified data partners.
Limitation: Not standard current measurement.
